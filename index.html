06 GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

The details
matter too.

Not sure where to start? These answers might help. You can always tell us about your project directly.

Let's discuss your idea ↗
How much does a business website cost? +

Our Basic website package starts at ₹12,000 for up to five responsive pages. Advanced, Growth and Premium packages start at ₹24,000, ₹43,500 and ₹55,000 respectively. Final pricing depends on the agreed features and work involved.

Can you set up a Shopify store? +

Yes. We can help with Shopify store structure, storefront design, product organization and integrations within the agreed scope. Shopify subscription and third-party app charges, if any, are separate.

Do you handle Etsy SEO and management? +

Yes. Our Etsy support can include store setup, product listing optimization, titles, descriptions, keyword planning, order workflows, customer support and advertising assistance, depending on the services selected.

How long does a project take? +

Timelines vary with the scope, content readiness, revisions and integrations. We provide an estimated schedule after reviewing your requirements rather than promising the same deadline for every project.

Is website maintenance available? +

Yes. Maintenance and support can be scoped to your needs. The Basic website package includes three months of support as outlined in your project agreement; other arrangements are quoted separately.

Can you optimize existing product listings? +

Yes. We can review product titles, descriptions, presentation and catalog structure. Search visibility depends on many factors, so rankings or sales cannot be guaranteed.

How does the free consultation work? +

Share a little about your business through the enquiry form. It opens a prepared WhatsApp message that you can review and send. We'll discuss your goals, possible scope and appropriate next steps.
